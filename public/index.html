    Study Mode rules

    1. Pick a specific study time. Once it starts you cannot pause, cancel or skip it.
    2. Your time is split into 25-minute focus blocks with 5-minute recall breaks.
    3. Stay fullscreen on this page. Switching apps or leaving fullscreen is a strike. 3 strikes and you are logged out.
    4. After each block, write what you studied in your own words. A thin note means you did not focus. Two in a row log you out.
    5. A good note gets a review date and time, scheduled for you.

    A web page cannot physically lock your phone. It detects leaving and penalises it.
