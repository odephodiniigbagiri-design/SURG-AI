Questions students ask.

Can Surg replace my textbooks and lectures?

No. Surg is a study partner for understanding and recall. It is for education only and gives no personal diagnosis or treatment advice.

Is Surg always accurate?

Surg is told never to invent statistics, doses, guidelines or references, but AI can still be wrong. Check important facts in your textbooks, and use thumbs-down to report a problem.

Where are my uploaded files kept?

In your own browser, not on our server. When you ask a question, the relevant excerpts are sent with it to the AI provider so Surg can answer.

What happens to my questions and ratings?

Questions pass through OpenRouter to the model provider. Thumbs ratings send a short snippet of the question and answer to the Surg team.

What happens when my trial ends?

AI features pause until you pay. Study Mode, your notes and reviewing existing flashcards keep working.

How do I pay?

Prices are in dollars and you pay in naira. Follow the payment instructions on the Plans page inside the app.
